When we founded TMP in 2009, we decided to do things differently. We saw organizations facing complex challenges were being offered simplistic, off-the-shelf solutions that didn’t match their problems.

We built a different approach from the ground up. From day one, we committed to fieldwork – not just analyzing data from afar, but meeting the people and seeing the places directly experiencing what our data was supposed to reflect.

At first, our primary focus was the climate, where we discovered a dangerous pattern of environmental instability preventing effective climate action, worsening impacts and creating cycles of increasing dysfunction. We identified a critical gap in near-term intelligence for the 2020s – the timeframe that actually matters for decisions being made today – and developed a world-class data analytics system to fill the void.

We branched out from there, concerned by how little attention was paid to the convergence of climate with existing social, economic and political pressures. Traditional analysis treated these factors in isolation, missing how they interact and amplify each other in real-world contexts. Without understanding these connections, even well-intentioned solutions often proved ineffective.

So we decided to apply our approach to those problems, and have been doing that ever since. By listening to your real needs rather than pushing predetermined solutions, we’ve been transforming these complex convergences into strategic advantages across more than 70 countries – and counting.
