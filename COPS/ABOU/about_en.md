Too often, analysts try to make your challenges fit their solutions. Rather than figuring out your goals and needs, they try to sell you data, analysis or solutions designed for someone else. That’s why the end products often sound rational and clear, but don’t actually work.

TMP is different: we know how to listen. Using what you tell us, we first figure out how environmental, economic, social and geopolitical forces might converge to impact you. While these convergences create many risks, our experience shows they often hold unrecognized opportunities too. It sounds simple, but our goal is to build solutions for your problems.

Doing that properly means we cover a lot of areas. Our proprietary climate analytics are a cornerstone, but we get asked to work on many topics: sovereign debt, critical supply chains, geostrategic conflicts, tropical deforestation and more. The sectors and places where we apply those solutions are equally diverse, from financial capitals to some of the world’s remotest communities.

TMP isn’t a one-size-fits-none kind of group, so we won’t pretend we can solve every problem. But if you’ve reached the place where traditional thinking reaches its limits – or are just tired of paying people who don’t engage with your specific problem – then get in touch to see if we can help.
