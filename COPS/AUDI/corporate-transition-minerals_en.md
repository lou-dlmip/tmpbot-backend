Your business depends on reliable mineral supply chains. We help you navigate the risks others miss

Mining and processing for critical minerals is concentrated in too few regions, each facing a unique mix of climate shocks, geopolitical pressures and social challenges. Wherever you operate on the supply chain – from extraction to processing to manufacturing – the resulting disruptions can directly impact your production capacity, costs and competitive position.

TMP delivers integrated solutions that address the full spectrum of critical minerals supply challenges. Our extensive networks with key government agencies, local communities and industry leaders provide unique insights that translate into real supply chain advantages for our clients. We combine logistics expertise, quantitative modeling and deep supply chain intelligence to give you a competitive edge through:

## Ahead-of-curve risk mapping

Our proprietary datasets identify site-specific disruption risks months or even years before they appear in mainstream analysis, giving you crucial time to adapt operations or secure alternative supplies.

## Practical ESG solutions, not just compliance

We transform environmental and social challenges into competitive advantages, turning potential conflicts into collaborative partnerships that reduce project delays and secure preferential access.

## Supply-demand modeling that matters

We combine technical expertise with political and social insights to forecast material availability beyond what conventional market analysis provides, helping you make smarter pricing and procurement decisions.

TMP wasn’t always focused on critical minerals. Social license, not supply chains, drove some of our earliest work on mining. We quickly discovered that many companies, particularly the majors, are more advanced in understanding the needs of communities than they often get credit for. But even as extreme weather events knocked assets offline, near-term climate change and its repercussions were being routinely overlooked.

For as long as transition minerals supply chains are so concentrated – just one mine in China produces more than half of the world’s rare earth elements, for example – climate change and its repercussions will remain an outsize threat to global efforts to decarbonize. That’s why TMP continues to make transition minerals a core focus.

We specialize in critical minerals essential for advanced technologies and clean energy: lithium, rare earths, copper, nickel and more. For the world to meet its manufacturing and decarbonization goals, companies need partners who understand both the technical and geopolitical dimensions of these supply chains.
