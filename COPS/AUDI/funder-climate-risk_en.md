TMP connects the dots between near-term climate risk and a range of important constituencies, delivering real progress in a unique way

Donors of all stripes recognize that addressing climate change requires engaging institutions with significant resources and influence, while at the same time delivering real change at the local and community level. Yet traditional climate approaches often fail to meaningfully involve key decision-makers in either camp.

TMP’s work bridges the gap in two critical ways. First, we’ve developed unique methodologies that translate climate science into the language of institutional and local decision-making. This approach has proven successful in engaging constituencies that typically remain on the sidelines of climate action – from communities in developing countries to corporations, investment firms, government agencies and militaries – by framing climate risks in terms directly relevant to their needs, wants, operations and objectives.

Second, we focus exclusively on the critical 2025-2035 timeframe, making climate change an immediate priority rather than a distant concern. Our analysis reveals the disruptions that are already locked in, regardless of future emissions scenarios, compelling institutions that might otherwise defer climate considerations to act.

Foundation support enables our work in several vital areas:

## Democratizing climate intelligence

Our research makes sophisticated climate risk analysis accessible to both underserved communities and powerful institutions, creating a common understanding of shared challenges.

## Building institutional capacity

We help organizations with significant resources – from defense departments to sovereign wealth funds – incorporate climate realities into their planning processes and capital allocation decisions.

## Fostering unusual alliances

Our convening platforms unite constituencies that rarely collaborate – bridging the needs of community advocates and military planners, or investors and environmental justice groups – creating powerful coalitions for climate resilience.

TMP has been focused on climate from day one. We launched after our founders witnessed firsthand how environmental instability was preventing climate action, leading to worsening impacts and cycles of increasing dysfunction in countries across the globe.

In the years since, we’ve developed funding mechanisms to help local communities reduce climate change risks while boosting local incomes, advised G7 governments on trade and industrial strategy, and spearheaded global efforts to bolster transition mineral supply chains against climate shocks by putting local resilience first.

Today, our proprietary near-term climate analytics are a cornerstone of our work, helping stakeholders everywhere prepare for a dramatic climate shift that will impact lives and livelihoods.
