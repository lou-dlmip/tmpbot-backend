Critical minerals shape investment returns. Our intelligence helps you navigate project risk, company exposure and strategic advantage

Whether you’re financing mining projects, investing in debt instruments, or managing equity positions in companies with significant minerals dependencies, your returns depend on more than geology and commodity prices. Critical minerals essential for clean energy and advanced technologies are concentrated in regions increasingly vulnerable to climate disruptions, geopolitical tensions and complex social dynamics.

TMP provides specialized intelligence that goes beyond conventional due diligence. Our multi-disciplinary approach integrates climate science, political risk assessment and supply chain expertise to help you identify value where others only see challenges. We translate complex risk dynamics into actionable investment signals through:

## Project-level risk assessment

Our proprietary analytics evaluate site-specific vulnerabilities that can delay development, interrupt operations or increase costs at mining projects, allowing you to price risk more accurately than competitors and avoid value-destroying surprises.

## Supply chain exposure mapping

We quantify how mineral dependencies affect downstream portfolio companies, identifying which operations are most vulnerable to disruption and which have built resilient sourcing strategies.

## Critical mineral pricing intelligence

Our models integrate geopolitical, regulatory and climate factors to provide more accurate forecasts of supply constraints and price volatility than conventional market analysis.

## Strategic positioning guidance

We help investors engage portfolio companies on securing preferential access to critical minerals through government relationships, community partnerships and alternative sourcing strategies.

TMP wasn’t always focused on critical minerals. Social license, not supply chains, drove some of our earliest work on mining. We quickly discovered that many companies, particularly the majors, are more advanced in understanding the needs of communities than they often get credit for. But even as extreme weather events knocked assets offline, near-term climate change and its repercussions were being routinely overlooked.

For as long as transition minerals supply chains are so concentrated – just one mine in China produces more than half of the world’s rare earth elements, for example – climate change and its repercussions will remain an outsize threat to global efforts to decarbonize. That’s why TMP continues to make transition minerals a core focus.

We specialize in minerals central to the energy transition, defence and security, and technological innovation: lithium, rare earths, copper, nickel and more. Get in touch to learn how we can strengthen your investment strategy in this critical but complex sector.
