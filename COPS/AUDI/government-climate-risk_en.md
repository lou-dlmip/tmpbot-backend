TMP connects the dots between near-term climate risk and public policy, helping governments identify vulnerabilities and develop resilient strategies

Public sector leaders everywhere recognize climate risk is an urgent threat to infrastructure, public health and fiscal stability. Yet traditional climate models fail to provide the actionable intelligence they need to effectively plan and allocate resources. TMP’s approach delivers superior insights in two key ways.

First, we don’t simply repackage existing climate models that aren’t suited to practical governance. Instead, we deliver analysis that accounts for the socioeconomic realities of your jurisdiction and translates climate exposure into specific impacts on critical services, public assets and community wellbeing.

Second, we focus exclusively on the critical 2025-2035 timeframe – aligning with electoral cycles, budget planning horizons and infrastructure development timelines. Our analysis quantifies the climate change that’s already locked in, regardless of future emissions scenarios, placing particular emphasis on events that could strain emergency services or trigger cascading system failures.

Our analytical and advisory services can help in several ways:

## Data and policy intelligence

Our jurisdiction-specific analysis measures climate shifts relative to local historical norms and integrates demographic, economic and critical infrastructure factors for comprehensive vulnerability assessments.

## Adaptation planning

We provide actionable insights that help prioritize resilience investments, optimize resource allocation and protect vulnerable communities against near-term climate impacts.

## Interagency coordination

Our proven methodologies facilitate whole-of-government approaches to climate preparedness, helping align various departments and levels of government around shared resilience objectives.

TMP has been focused on climate from day one. We launched after our founders witnessed firsthand how environmental instability was preventing climate action, leading to worsening impacts and cycles of increasing dysfunction in countries across the globe.

In the years since, we’ve developed geospatial risk assessment tools used by some of the biggest companies and investors in the world, advised G7 governments on trade and industrial strategy, and spearheaded global efforts to bolster transition mineral supply chains against extreme weather events and other climate shocks.

Today, our proprietary near-term climate analytics are a cornerstone of our work, helping policymakers prepare for a dramatic climate shift that will impact lives and livelihoods.
