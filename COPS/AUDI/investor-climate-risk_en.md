TMP connects the dots between near-term climate risk and business strategy, helping clients identify emerging threats and find opportunity in uncertainty

Asset managers everywhere recognize climate risk as a material factor impacting near-term performance and valuations. Yet traditional climate models fail to provide the actionable intelligence needed for today’s investment decisions. TMP’s approach delivers superior insights in two key ways.

First, we don’t simply repackage existing climate models that aren’t suited to quantitative investment analysis. Instead, we deliver proprietary metrics that account for market dynamics and translate climate exposure into specific impacts on asset classes, sectors and return profiles.

Second, we focus exclusively on the critical 2025-2035 timeframe – aligning with institutional investment horizons, regulatory requirements, mark-to-market realities and similar considerations. Our analysis quantifies the climate change that’s already locked in, regardless of future emissions scenarios, placing particular emphasis on its ability to create tail risks and volatility events.

Our analytical and advisory services can help in several ways:

## Data and market intelligence

Our location-specific analysis measures climate shifts relative to local historical norms – not global averages – and integrates economic, social, political and demographic factors to provide a complete risk picture.

## Investment strategy

We provide actionable insights beyond standard ESG data, enabling you to identify mispriced climate risk across asset classes and incorporate these signals into your existing investment frameworks.

## Fiduciary reporting

Our proven methodologies help investors satisfy evolving disclosure requirements and communicate physical climate risk exposure to clients, beneficiaries and regulators with greater precision.

TMP has been focused on climate from day one. We launched after our founders witnessed firsthand how environmental instability was preventing climate action, leading to worsening impacts and cycles of increasing dysfunction in countries across the globe.

In the years since, we’ve developed geospatial risk assessment tools used by some of the biggest companies and investors in the world, advised G7 governments on trade and industrial strategy, and spearheaded global efforts to bolster transition mineral supply chains against extreme weather events and other climate shocks.

Today, our proprietary near-term climate analytics are a cornerstone of our work, helping investors across sectors and borders prepare for a dramatic climate shift that will occur in the next 10 years.
