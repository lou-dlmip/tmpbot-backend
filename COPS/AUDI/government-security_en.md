A changing climate is creating new imperatives for military readiness. TMP’s data and analysis enable strategic adaptation and operational resilience

Global security stands at a critical juncture. From Europe’s accelerated rearmament to emerging power competition, defense establishments face complex threat matrices. While countering traditional adversaries remains paramount, strategists must now integrate a new variable: climate destabilization.

Over the next decade, climate-intensified disruptions will simultaneously compromise military supply chains and increase operational demands for humanitarian assistance and disaster response capabilities. These dual pressures require a new kind of strategic recalibration and planning.

TMP delivers precision climate intelligence and integrated security environment assessment capabilities that enable proactive defense planning and resource allocation.

We collaborate with respected security institutions to provide:

## Supply chain resilience

Vulnerability assessments of critical mineral supply chains and defense industrial base infrastructure with site-specific risk profiles.

## Threat environment analysis

Targeted analysis of climate impacts on operational environments – including adversary territories (Russia, China) – identifying specific windows of heightened risk.

## Deployment forecasting

Forward deployment forecasting for humanitarian assistance and disaster relief operations, with implications for force structure and readiness requirements.

## Exercises and games

Strategic convening of cross-sector security stakeholders to align capabilities and identify action pathways for alliance strengthening.

TMP has worked on conflict and security dynamics since 2011. Back then, our focus was on understanding how climate interacted with local conflict and social license to operate. Landscope, a geospatial analytics tool for evaluating conflict risks across emerging markets, is a prime example of this work.

Our focus evolved after we launched our critical minerals workstream – a project that brought us into closer contact with security and defense decision-makers, who rely on the same mineral supply chains as stakeholders working on the transition to green energy. We quickly realized that climate destabilization was widely underappreciated by this group, and sensed an opportunity to shift mindsets – and resource allocation – around this crucial topic.

Today, we’re working with partners including RUSI, the world’s oldest and the UK’s leading defense and security think tank, the Atlantic Council, the UK Ministry of Defence and others on issues ranging from critical mineral supply chains to humanitarian assistance and disaster relief.

Climate is now a core security concern. TMP’s unique data and expert analysis are helping governments and defense leaders adjust. Contact us to learn more.
