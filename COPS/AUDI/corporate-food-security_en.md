The global food system is under pressure. Navigate risks – and seize a competitive advantage – with TMP

From the crops we consume to the places we grow them, global food systems are too concentrated. No matter where on the supply chain – or in the world – you operate, a combination of climate shocks, geopolitical pressures and social challenges could be coming for your bottom line.

TMP’s supply chain analysis can map your risk exposure. Highly localized and focused exclusively on the 2025-2035 timeframe, our proprietary climate data can tell you which commodities are most threatened – and where. With our background in social and geopolitical risk, we can anticipate price crises and other shocks that other analysts miss.

We focus on:

## Risk-oriented modeling

While other climate models emphasize long-term, global trends, TMP’s proprietary data focuses squarely on the here and now, layering in social, environmental and security risk.

## Timeframes that matter

We focus on timeframes that matter to decisionmakers, providing insights into seasonal shifts that are hugely influential for agriculture.

## Scenario exercises

Our scenario exercises help businesses and other stakeholders plan for situations across the spectrum of probability and risk.

## Commodity analysis

Our experts don’t just map the risks – they propose concrete solutions. That includes intelligence on adaptation strategies and climate-resilient crops.

TMP’s interest in food security goes back to our earliest work in farm resilience and social risk. It deepened in 2022 after Russia invaded Ukraine and the prospect of shortages sent wheat prices soaring to record highs. Our data tells us extreme weather events linked to near-term climate change are poised to drive similar market volatility. And this time, it won’t be temporary.

Supply chain resilience processes are effective at responding to one-off, time-bound challenges – not fundamental shifts in availability and affordability. For that, a new outlook – underpinned by better information – will be required.
