Climate change is reshaping humanitarian challenges. TMP’s specialized insights help foundations maximize impact by engaging across sectors

The philanthropic landscape is evolving rapidly to combat accelerating climate impacts. Forward-thinking foundations recognize that effective climate action requires unprecedented collaboration across traditional boundaries – including with security and military institutions, who increasingly acknowledge climate change as a threat multiplier.

TMP is working to bridge these worlds. Our collaborations with defense establishments focus on energy transition, conflict prevention and humanitarian response coordination – areas where philanthropic and security interests increasingly converge.

This cross-sector approach yields several benefits for our foundation partners:

## Comprehensive risk understanding

Our security sector expertise provides foundations with insights into climate-security dynamics that inform more effective program design, particularly in vulnerable regions.

## Expanded impact pathways

By understanding military priorities around renewable energy adoption and operational resilience, foundations can identify unexpected leverage points for accelerating climate solutions.

## Enhanced coordination

Our relationships with security institutions create opportunities for dialogue between humanitarian organizations and military responders, which is critical for effective crisis management in climate-affected regions.

## Systems change acceleration

Working across institutional boundaries allows foundations to influence resource allocation decisions well beyond traditional philanthropic spheres.

TMP has worked on conflict and security dynamics since 2011. Back then, our focus was on understanding how climate interacted with local conflict and social license to operate. Landscope, a geospatial analytics tool for evaluating conflict risks across emerging markets, is a prime example of this work.

Our focus evolved after we launched our critical minerals workstream – a project that brought us into closer contact with security and defense decision-makers, who rely on the same mineral supply chains as stakeholders working on the transition to green energy. We quickly realized that climate destabilization was widely underappreciated by this group, and sensed an opportunity to shift mindsets – and resource allocation – around this crucial topic.

Today, we’re working with partners including RUSI, the world’s oldest and the UK’s leading defense and security think tank, the Atlantic Council, the UK Ministry of Defence and others on issues ranging from critical mineral supply chains to humanitarian assistance and disaster relief.

Our unique position at the intersection of climate science, security analysis and humanitarian response enables foundations to deploy resources more strategically. To learn more, or to find out how to join the conversation, get in touch with our team.
