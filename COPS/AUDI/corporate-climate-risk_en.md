TMP connects the dots between near-term climate risk and business strategy, helping clients identify emerging threats and find opportunity in uncertainty

Businesses everywhere are coming to realize climate risk is a strategic, near-term problem. But traditional climate models often fail to deliver actionable insights for today’s decision-makers. TMP’s approach is fundamentally different in two ways.

First, we don’t just repackage existing climate models that aren’t suited to business decision-making. Instead, we deliver analysis that acknowledges the complexity of your operating environment and connects climate realities to your specific assets, operations and growth plans.

Second, we focus exclusively on the critical 2025-2035 timeframe – the period that aligns with your strategic planning cycles. Our analysis captures the climate change that’s already locked in, regardless of future emissions scenarios, paying special attention to extreme events that truly disrupt operations. Our analytical and advisory services include:

## Data and market intelligence

Our location-specific analysis measures climate shifts relative to local historical norms – not global averages – and integrates economic, social, political and demographic factors to provide a complete risk picture.

## Strategic planning

We provide context-rich insights that go beyond raw climate data, helping you understand how environmental changes interact with your specific business environment and objectives.

## Social license

Our proven approaches to working with communities help companies earn lasting social license to operate. Our collaborative platforms unite companies, investors, NGOs and governments around shared climate resilience goals.

TMP has been focused on climate from day one. We launched after our founders witnessed firsthand how environmental instability was preventing climate action, leading to worsening impacts and cycles of increasing dysfunction in countries across the globe.

In the years since, we’ve developed geospatial risk assessment tools used by some of the biggest companies and investors in the world, advised G7 governments on trade and industrial strategy, and spearheaded global efforts to bolster transition mineral supply chains against extreme weather events and other climate shocks.

Today, our proprietary near-term climate analytics are a cornerstone of our work, helping business leaders across sectors and borders prepare for a dramatic climate shift that will impact their bottom line in the next 10 years.
