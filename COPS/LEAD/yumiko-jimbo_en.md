Yumiko Jimbo is the Managing Director of TMP Climate, the for-profit arm of TMP. Before joining in 2018, she built a career spanning more than 20 years in end-to-end design and manufacturing management with the Sumitomo Group, Mitsubishi Group and other companies, leading quality control programs for factories across Asia and Europe. She also lived in Thailand, where she studied the language and later worked in the automotive industry. Yumiko holds a BA in Luso-Brazilian Studies from Tenri University. She is based in Japan.
